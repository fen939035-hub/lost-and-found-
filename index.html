How It Works

Simple steps for using MNHS Lost & Found.

1. Register

Create an account using your name and username.

2. Report

Submit details about your lost or found item.

3. Approval

The administrator checks and approves the report.

4. Browse

Approved reports become visible to users.

5. Claim

If you recognize your item, submit a claim.

6. Resolution

The administrator reviews the claim and resolves the item when appropriate.

About MNHS Lost & Found

Our Purpose


The MNHS Lost & Found system was created to provide a more organized way of handling lost and found belongings within Marangal National High School.


Instead of depending only on handwritten records, bulletin board announcements, or verbal reports, the system provides a centralized web-based platform for reporting and searching items.
